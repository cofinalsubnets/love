Privacy policy

This policy covers love, including the love app for Android. Last updated 6 November 2026.

love does not collect, store or share your personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it runs no server that receives your data.

On your device

The app keeps its files, such as programs, settings and saved games, in its own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

Network. The app can use the network because love is a programming system and programs you run may use it. love contacts only what you or a program you run asks it to. Those connections are not made to us.

Microphone. The microphone is only used if you allow it, and only by programs that listen, such as the tuner. The sound is handled on your device. love does not save it or send it anywhere.

A program you write or choose to run can do things love itself does not, including sending data elsewhere. This policy cannot speak for software that does not come with love.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles that install under its own privacy policy and may give developers summary statistics, such as install counts and crash reports.

Children

love does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will appear on this page. Its history is in the public source repository.
