Privacy policy

This covers love, including the love app for Android. Last updated 7 November 2026.

love collects no personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends nothing to us.

The app keeps its files in its own storage on your device. Uninstalling it or clearing its data removes them.

The microphone is used only if you allow it, by programs that listen, such as the tuner. The sound stays on your device and is never saved or sent. The network is used only for what you, or a program you run, ask for. A program you write or run can do things love itself does not.
